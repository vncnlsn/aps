Who We Are

Built on
Decades of Field Expertise

AllState Permit Services has been the trusted standard in permit acquisition for the oil and gas industry since 1980. We don't just coordinate — we embed ourselves in every project with direct field oversight, dedicated operations supervisors, and agents who know the land.

Our team has navigated federal agencies, municipal jurisdictions, wetlands, tribal lands, and everything in between — from the Gulf Coast to the Northern Rockies. When crews need to move, we make sure the permits are already there.
