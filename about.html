Leadership

Ownership stays close to every serious decision.

This is a small-company advantage. Clients get the decision-makers, not a handoff chain.

MF

Founder & CEO

Mark Fischbuch

Mark founded Allstate Permit Services with the belief that successful projects are built on communication, accountability, and long-term relationships. His leadership style should read as steady and practical: the kind of owner who stays close enough to understand the details without losing sight of the bigger picture.

He has shaped the company around the idea that clients should always know who is accountable, what comes next, and how the work is being handled. That discipline is part of the brand.

ZN

President of Operations & CFO

Zane Nelson

Zane brings the operational focus that keeps the company moving cleanly through scheduling, oversight, and financial discipline. He should feel like the person who turns a good plan into reliable execution.

His role in the mockup is to reinforce the company’s credibility: careful coordination, clear systems, and enough structure to support both growth and consistency without losing the personal feel of a smaller team.
