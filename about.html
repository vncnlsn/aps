Company History

Built From the Field Up

AllState Permit Services was founded in 1980 with a simple but unwavering mission: provide the oil and gas industry with permit acquisition services that are faster, more thorough, and more accountable than anything else available.

That commitment hasn't changed in 45 years. What has changed is our reach. From our early roots in the Western United States, APS has grown into a national operation with proven results across 27 states — navigating federal agencies, tribal lands, municipalities, wetlands, and everything in between.

Our growth has always been organic, driven by client relationships and project referrals. When a seismic crew doesn't get delayed because permits were already secured, they remember who made that happen. That's been our competitive advantage since day one.

1980

Founded

AllState Permit Services is established to serve the oil and gas industry's growing demand for professional, accountable permit acquisition in the field.

Late 1980s – 1990s

Expanding the Footprint

APS expands operations across the southern and central United States, developing deep expertise in Texas — eventually completing work in 128 individual counties within the state alone.

2000s

GIS Integration

Recognizing the transformative power of geographic information systems for permitting work, APS builds an in-house GIS team capable of delivering comprehensive surface, mineral, and infrastructure mapping databases for every project.

2010s

National Operations — 27 States

APS completes 2D, 3D, 4D, and micro-seismic projects across 27 states, from the Gulf Coast to the Northern Rockies. Right of way acquisition and title curative services are fully integrated into the service offering.

Today

The Standard Continues

With 45 years of field experience behind us and a focused, expert team, APS continues to deliver the timeliness, accountability, and cost-effectiveness that has defined us since 1980.
