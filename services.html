01 · Seismic permitting

Permitting work that keeps exploration schedules realistic.

What it covers

  • Route and access coordination
  • Permit management and submissions
  • Stakeholder and agency communication
  • Project timing support

How APS helps

APS should read as the team that keeps the moving pieces aligned: helping the client understand what is possible, what needs to change, and how to get the work done without creating unnecessary friction.

02 · Right-of-way acquisition

Right-of-way acquisition built around trust and timing.

What it covers

  • Easements and access agreements
  • Landowner communication
  • Compensation coordination
  • Documentation and tracking

How APS helps

In this kind of work, tone matters. APS should present itself as respectful, calm, and solution-oriented, helping the project move while protecting the relationship with the landowner.

03 · Title curative

Title curative and parcel cleanup.

What it covers

  • Ownership research
  • Issue identification and resolution
  • Curative document preparation
  • Project file cleanup

How APS helps

Title work can slow everything down if it is messy. APS should show that it understands how to clear obstacles efficiently and keep the rest of the project from stalling.

04 · GIS support

GIS support that makes the project easier to understand.

What it covers

  • Maps and route visualization
  • Parcel overlays and data organization
  • Project reporting support
  • Data cleanup and consistency checks

How APS helps

GIS should not feel decorative. It should be useful, clear, and practical enough to help both internal teams and outside stakeholders see what is happening on the ground.

05 · Landowner relations

Landowner relations and project coordination.

What it covers

  • Outreach and status communication
  • Scheduling and site coordination
  • Issue resolution and follow-up
  • Support for clients and field agents

How APS helps

This is where the company can stand out the most. Good landowner work is respectful, steady, and honest. It is not about pressure. It is about keeping projects moving while treating people fairly.
